About

// 01

I'm the connective tissue between engineers, designers, content folk, and the people paying for it all.

For the last five years I've helped small, fully-remote product teams ship things they're proud of, most of that time at QalbyApp, a faith-based mobile product where I owned a three-year roadmap across an app, two websites, and a community portal. Most recently I was a project delivery manager at Siiru Tech, a regional SaaS startup, where the work covered the product side too: translating business goals into PRDs, sprint plans, and the kind of unglamorous follow-ups that keep things moving. I'm now looking for my next remote role.

I work best where the work is values-driven, the timezones are forgiving, and the team treats clarity as a kindness. On the side I built and shipped TaskSpin, a spin-the-wheel to-do list for mornings when the inbox feels louder than the brain. It was my first personal project to make it all the way to live, and it started a habit: most weekends there's another small build on the go. A small reminder that calm and play can sit at the same desk.
